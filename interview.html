General Interview Tips

  • Power pose for 2 minutes before the interview — it boosts confidence and reduces stress.
  • Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral questions.
  • Pause briefly before answering — it shows confidence and thoughtfulness.
  • Research the company’s mission and values.
  • Practice speaking slowly; nerves make people rush.
  • Prepare 2–3 thoughtful questions to ask the interviewer.
