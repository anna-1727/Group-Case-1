What is Information Systems?

The connection between technology and business.

Information Systems professionals use technology, data, and business strategy to solve real organizational problems. That combination gives IS students the flexibility to pursue both highly technical careers and business-focused technology roles.

One degree gives you access to careers on both sides of the technology and business world.
